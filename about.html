About this site

I made this movie review site for my COAP 2000 Web Design class. In lieu of my own reviews and summaries, I have supplanted some from across the internet, which I have cited below. I knew some HTML before starting, but making this site taught me many things about how CSS works. I struggled a lot at first, but I think I'm happy with how it turned out. It will be a good stepping stone for any sites I decide to make in the future.
